The Game

Risk 42 translates the full classic Risk arc — random deal, army build-up, continent income, conquest — onto the standard dartboard. Where T&T is a timed sprint over 20 wedge-territories with no armies, Risk 42 is the marathon: 40 box-territories, army stacks, a continent economy, and two endgames.

The premise: 42 = 42 → 40 + 2

The classic board has 42 territories. Excluding Japan and Madagascar (the two single-connection islands) leaves exactly 40 — matching the board's 40 single boxes (20 inner + 20 outer). The two bulls are not territories; they are the Arsenal. The world was fitted onto the board by proximity projection (bull = the Black Sea) with hard anchors Iceland → outer-5 and South Africa → outer-3.

The crowd

Same mixed table as T&T: one shark, casuals, everyone must matter until the end. Balancing remains social — the leader's fat stacks sit on famous wedges and the table guns for them. Design guardrail: dead players keep playing (see Exile).

Rules of Play

The Deal. The app shuffles all 40 boxes and deals them exactly equally — 2p: 20/20 · 3p: 13 each (1 blank) · 4p: 10 each · 5p: 8 each · 6p: 6 each (4 blanks). Every dealt box starts with 2 armies owned by its player. Leftover boxes stay blank and are claimable in-game by the normal deposit rules (single claims with 1, a treble/double founds its fed box with 2) — the blanks are the opening land-rush. Who starts: the players decide — no app mechanism; setup takes turn order as input.
Deposits (the base verb). A turn is 3 darts (plus continent income). Every dart deposits armies:
  • Single (inner or outer fat box): ±1 into the box it lands in
  • Treble: ±2 into that wedge's inner box (the supply line runs inward)
  • Double: ±2 into that wedge's outer box
Plus on blank/own boxes (claim bigger / reinforce), minus on enemy boxes (damage). Every box has exactly two feeders — one +1 direct, one +2 ring — income per box is identical across the board by construction.
Combat — mirror damage. Your dart's value goes in the box: up if it's yours or blank, down if it's theirs. A box reduced to 0 flips to the attacker with 1 army. Mid-turn flips chain: capture with one dart, reinforce your conquest with the next. Overkill is wasted (a treble into a 1-army box flips it; the extra damage evaporates). There are no caps, no dice, no defender throws.
Exile & clawback. Zero boxes ≠ dead. An exiled player keeps the full 3-dart turn every round — pure offense (nothing of theirs to reinforce) — and any box they knock to 0 becomes theirs again at 1 army. Death costs defensive income, not the seat. Works identically in both end modes.
The Arsenal (bull charge). A bull dart charges the rest of your turn: single bull (25) = +1, double bull (50) = +2 to every dart thrown after it, this turn only. Charges stack additively (25 then 50 = +3 on the last dart). A charged dart deposits/removes its ring value plus the charge. A bull with no darts left behind it fizzles. Load, then fire — aiming bull first telegraphs the raid.
Continent income. While you hold every box of a continent, you get +1 dart per turn. Asia pays +2 (11 boxes — superpower money). Armies ≈ darts is the honest Risk translation of reinforcement income.
UI REQUIREMENT — pre-turn dart budget. The scorer must show the player their exact dart count for the upcoming turn before the turn starts, with its sources (base 3 · +1 SA · +2 Asia · …). Never let the budget be discovered mid-throw.
Endgames.
  • Domination (headline default) — hold all 40 boxes (blanks must be claimed too). The classic coronation; exile rules keep the last hold-out fighting.
  • Clock (alt) — equal turns per player, branded 170 / 301 / 501. At the horn: 1 pt per box + continent bonuses (defaults below). Full ranking, tiebreak = one sudden-death dart each, nearest bull.

Screens & UI

Scorer — first screen (the dartboard)

Board. The match UI shows the dartboard as risk-dart-board.svg: numbers, labels, wires — with the 40 single boxes tinted in the same colors as their territories on the world map (risk-territory-board.svg palette). Build step: join the map's fill-layer shades to named territories by centroid containment → emit a territory→fill palette JSON; the board component consumes it. Doubles/trebles stay traditional red/green (supply lines), bull stays bull.
Avatars. Small avatar chips (player color + initials/emoji) mark who owns each box, with the army count. Positioned at box centers (inner r≈68 / outer r≈160 polar positions — same math as the labels).
Turn chrome. Pre-turn budget banner (sources listed), Arsenal charge indicator (⚡ +1/+2 while active), war log feed, standings strip, exile badge for landless players.

TV — second screen (the world map)

Cast view. The second screen renders risk-territory-board.svg — the actual Risk map — with live ownership tinting and the same avatar chips per territory, current player highlight, continent control summary. URL + tab-cast + ~1 s polling, public-by-link — same pattern as the existing TV spectator mode. State source: the server-persisted game record (write-through per dart, own table + uuid — the conquest persistence pattern).

Implementation

Engine (M1)

Pure TypeScript at src/lib/game/risk-engine.ts — no Svelte imports, modelled on conquest-engine.ts. TDD-first (red → green → refactor, the house rule).

  • createGame(playerIds, { mode: 'domination' | 'clock', clockTurns? }) — shuffles the deal (equal split, blanks, 2 armies), seeds RNG for reproducible tests
  • applyDart(state, segment, multiplier) — one dart: Arsenal charge register (bull-first, additive, turn-scoped) · deposit/claim/reinforce/mirror-damage · capture-at-0 flips 1 · mid-turn chains · turn lifecycle (budget = 3 + continent bonus)
  • standings(state) — boxes, continents held, army totals, score (clock mode)
  • Win detection: all-40 owned (Domination) · horn + ranking + nearest-bull tiebreak (Clock)
  • Acceptance: unit suite + seeded multi-game invariant sweep (both modes, 2–6 players), like T&T's 40-game sweep

Parked knobs (deliberately out of v1)

  • Bull events — artillery strikes / wildcard deposits / earthquake were alternatives to the Arsenal; the bulls now have a job, but the socket exists if playtests want an event valve.
  • Shanghai-style feat — T&T momentum reward; not ported yet.
  • Continent income sizing — flat +1 (Asia +2) is the default; per-continent tables if it plays flat.
  • Clock scoring values — assumption lock #7.
  • Final name — working title "Risk 42" (the 42-territory premise). Naming pass comes last, per house tradition.
