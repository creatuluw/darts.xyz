01

The Game

Risk on a dartboard. Multiple players fight for the 20 wedge-territories; the dartboard's own number ring becomes a fixed world map of six continents.

Design pillars — locked in interview

  • Mixed crowd by design. One shark, three casuals, everyone matters until the buzzer. Settling is hard (trebles), stealing is cheap (singles) — sharks become settlers, casuals become raiders.
  • The leader gets ganged up on. Balancing is social, not mechanical — the table aims at whoever is winning.
  • Zero dead throws (almost). Every dart attacks, claims or reinforces. The one exception is deliberate: singles into blank land do nothing, because the frontier is treble-only.
  • Death is drama, not exile. Landless players hunt the bull altar; resurrection can trigger a one-dart duel against their neighbour.
  • Momentum has a name: Shanghai. Single + double + treble of one number in a turn = one extra dart next turn. Rare, loud, precious.
Decision log Claim-based ownership and a heat/streak economy were explored and rejected — this spec is the treble-frontier + siege model, finalized with the bull altar, fixed continents, timed wars and Shanghai bonus.

Rules of Play

The complete ruleset as locked. Everything below is implemented in a deterministic game engine (see §06) — no referee judgment calls anywhere.

The map

  • 20 territories — the wedges 1–20. Each has an owner (or blank) and 0–3 HP.
  • The bull is not a territory. It is the Altar — only landless players throw at it (see Death & Resurrection).
  • 6 fixed continents, always the same, always the same colour:

A turn

  • 3 darts, fixed seat rotation. Shanghai bonus darts are added on top (see below).
  • Whatever the dart hits, resolves immediately — mid-turn ownership changes are legal and matter.
Dart lands on…SegmentEffect
Blank wedgeSingle / DoubleNothing. The frontier is treble-only — wild land is civilized by treble or not at all.
Blank wedgeTrebleFound it. Territory is yours at full 3 HP.
Enemy wedgeSingle / Double / Treble1 / 2 / 3 damage. At 0 HP → captured, arrives at 1 HP (fresh conquests are fragile).
Own wedgeAnyReinforce +1 HP (cap 3).
Bull — you own land25 / 50Nothing. The altar ignores the living.
Bull — you are dead25 / 50Resurrection flow — see Death & Resurrection.

Shanghai

  • Single + double + treble of the same number, same turn, any order → +1 dart next turn (4 total).
  • The feat counts regardless of what the land does: on enemy ground S+D+T = 6 damage = guaranteed capture (with a mid-turn flip, later darts reinforce your new land); on your own full-HP land it is pure form — extra dart earned anyway.
  • Stacks in theory, not in practice (~1-in-100-turns event even when hunted deliberately). No anti-stacking rules needed.
  • Dead players on the altar cannot Shanghai (the bull has no segments).

Death & Resurrection — the Bull Altar

A player with 0 territories is dead. Their whole turn is thrown at the bull (all 3 darts). One hit (outer 25 or inner 50) triggers:

  1. Blank territories exist → the dead player must claim one blank of their choice (strategy: complete your continent, block the leader's). It arrives at 1 HP. Remaining darts continue as normal attacks.
  2. No blanks → the dead player robs the next living player in seat order (skipping other dead players; the victim must own ≥1 territory). Attacker picks which territory. The duel: the victim immediately throws one save dart at the bull —
    • Victim hits → DENIED. Territory kept (HP unchanged), attacker stays dead and restarts the bull hunt next turn.
    • Victim misses → theft succeeds at 1 HP. Remaining attacker darts continue as normal attacks — you're back and angry.
  3. Miss all three bulls → still dead, try again next turn. No penalty, just tension.
Design note · graveyard politics Resurrection targets are fixed by seating: your resurrection victim is whoever throws after you. Whoever sits after the weakest player gets farmed. Accepted for v1 (matches are long enough to forgive); lazy fix if it stings: re-seat between matches.

Clock mode (default) — scoring

  • Each player throws a fixed budget of darts (presets in §03, default 301). The app truncates the final turn if the budget isn't a multiple of 3 — e.g. the 301st dart can literally be the last dart of the match.
  • When the last dart falls: 1 point per territory, +2 per complete continent. Full ranking 1st/2nd/3rd/…
  • Tiebreak: one sudden-death dart each, nearest bull wins (inner beats outer; a miss is out; repeat if still level).
  • Dead ≠ out. A landless player at the buzzer simply scores what they hold (possibly 0) — but they were never benched.

Domination mode (selectable alternative)

  • First player to hold 7 territories or any one complete continent wins instantly. A 5–15 minute sprint; same combat, altar and Shanghai rules, no clock.

Edge rules (engine must implement)

  • Capture mid-turn: the remaining darts of that turn apply to the new owner (your attack became a reinforce).
  • The duel save dart is free — it never counts against the defender's dart budget.
  • Multiple dead players resolve their altar turns independently; duels never chain (a dead victim can't be robbed — they own nothing).
  • Domination instant-win is checked after every dart resolves.

Match Lengths — Presets

A match is a fixed dart budget per player. Ten presets, named like the classic x01 games. Durations assume ~30 s per 3-dart turn (app-scored); halve the pace assumption for drunken nights, and remember every preset persists — a long war can be resumed any night.

The Commentator

An LLM war-commentator writes a line every 2 turns into the Commentary tab. Voice: PDC darts-announcer urgency crossed with dry British pub wit, seasoned with TV-chef quotes — and Muppet Show sound stingers where it makes sense.

Persona spec (system prompt draft)

You are the live commentator for "Trebles and Territories", a Risk-style
darts conquest game. Voice: PDC darts commentator urgency (Sid Waddell
school) crossed with dry British pub wit, seasoned liberally with TV-chef
quotes (Gordon Ramsay, Jamie Oliver, the Swedish Chef).

Rules of your booth:
- 2 to 4 punchy sentences. No markdown. At most one emoji.
- React ONLY to the actual game state you are given: captures, sieges,
  altar duels, deaths, Shanghais, standings, darts remaining.
- When a territory is founded, captured or defended: one chef quote or
  cooking metaphor. Bork bork bork is always in season.
- End with at most ONE sound cue tag, only where it makes sense:
  [sound:swedish-chef] [sound:animal] [sound:statler-waldorf]
  [sound:piggy] [sound:kermit] [sound:trumpet]  — or no tag at all.
- Never invent throws that are not in the state.

Integration — verified

  x-opencode-session: conquest-<matchId>            // stable per match → routing + prompt caching
  User-Agent:      darts-xyz-conquest/1.0           // required, identify the app
Body:  { model: "glm-5.3-flash", temperature: 0.9, max_tokens: 400,
         messages: [ system(persona), user(compact game state JSON) ] }
  • Trigger: server-side after every 2nd completed turn (turnCount % 2 === 0) — roughly once a minute at 30 s turns. Well inside GLM-5.3-Flash limits (1,580 requests / 5 h).
  • Reasoning model: GLM-5.3-Flash emits reasoning_content before content — parse choices[0].message.content, ignore the reasoning field, give it headroom (max_tokens ≥ 400).
  • State payload: players + standings, 20-territory map with owners/HP, last 6 events, mode, darts remaining. ~400 tokens.
  • Failure policy: never block play — 8 s timeout, silent skip, log. The feed survives without the model.

Implementation

Fits the existing stack untouched: SvelteKit 2 + Svelte 5 runes + TypeScript, Drizzle/PostgreSQL (darts schema), REST under /api, ElevenLabs soundboard + Kokoro TTS. New game mode beside — never inside — the x01 engine.

Game engine — pure & deterministic

src/lib/game/conquest-engine.ts, modelled on the existing match-engine.ts: a pure reducer, no I/O, fully unit-testable.

// shape (summary)
type Phase = 'turn' | 'duel_save' | 'tiebreak' | 'finished';
interface ConquestState {
  mode: 'clock' | 'domination';
  preset: 51|101|151|201|251|301|…|1501;          // step 50, always an x01 number, 301 = default
  players: { id: string; seat: number; dartsThrown: number; bonusDarts: number }[];
  seatOrder: string[];                 // graveyard politics live here

}
applyDart(state, segment): { state, events }   // segment: 'T20' | 'D5' | 'S12' | 'BULL' | 'DBULL' | 'MISS'
applyDuelSave(state, segment): { state, events }

07

The Morning After — Recap Videos

A separate feature: every finished match becomes a ~60-second recap film the next day, authored by an LLM agent using HyperFrames (HTML-based video composition — showcase · skills). Players who have an email address on file receive a link. Nobody has to do anything — the war comes back to you at breakfast.

Pipeline — daily, fully async

  1. Trigger (cron ~10:00): find conquest_matches ended ≥ 1 day ago with no recap row → queue one.
  2. Script pass — GLM-5.3-Flash (same verified endpoint as §05, persona continuity): select 5–8 beats from the turn/event log + commentary highlights, write captions and narration lines, place stinger tags.
  3. Author pass — agent with HyperFrames skills (hyperframes-core, keyframes, audio): builds the composition from the recap template — the §04 state board as hero visual, map-timeline animation, kinetic-type captions. Seek-safe GSAP only; per-match content injected via HyperFrames variables (names, colours, scores, beats) — the same source renders every match.
  4. Audio: ElevenLabs narrator voiceover (existing pipeline), music bed carved under the VO (data-fx-carve), Muppet stingers where the script says so.
  5. Render: HeyGen cloud render (preferred) or hyperframes CLI local fallback → MP4 1080p. Quality gates before render: lint · check · keyframes · snapshot.
  6. Deliver: store under a tokenized unlisted URL; email the link to every player with an email on file; per-player opt-out. Link-only — never attachments.

Reliability ladder Agent-authored bespoke film (best) → fixed template + variables (always works) → no video, no email (never blocks anything). The recap is a day-after courtesy — a failed render costs a shrug, not a feature. Render gates from the HyperFrames skill contract (lint, check, snapshots at proof times) run before any render is accepted.
