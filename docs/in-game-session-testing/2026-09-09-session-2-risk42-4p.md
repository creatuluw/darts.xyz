## 5. Feedback / improvements

- **R1 · P2 — No commentary on Risk 42 TV** (see §3) — port the TvCommentary pipeline; it's the mode that most needs a narrator.
- **R2 · P2 — Setup card click feels dead in automation** (and possibly on touch): the Risk 42 format card needed a direct DOM click while the T&T card responded normally to element clicks — worth a manual check on mobile/TV browsers.
- **R3 · P3 — No dart-remaining pips vs budget on the TV**: the TV shows standings + map but not the current player's dart budget/charge; adding "VOLT · 2/3 darts · Arsenal +1" would make spectating turns easier.
- **R4 · P3 — War log has no turn separators** across 4 players; a subtle "— PT's turn —" divider (or per-player color bar) would help following the feed.
- **R5 · P3 — Arsenal charge isn't visible on the scorer board** during the charged turn (only in state); a small ⚡+2 badge near the turn banner would sell the moment.
- **R6 · Design question — army stacking is uncapped** (17-inner hit 14 armies); fine by design, but a leaderboard "biggest stack" callout would turn inflation into spectacle.
- **R7 · Praise**: the exile banner copy is excellent ("In exile — capture any box at 0 armies to claw back") — it teaches the rule exactly when it matters. The budget banner with itemized continent sources is the best rules-communication element in the app.

## 6. Fun assessment

- **Table-shoots-the-leader works as designed**: JO went 10 → 0 boxes under a 4-player coalition, got exiled, and clawed back to 3 in a single turn — the arc the mode was designed around, and it played dramatically at the table.
- **Income strategy is legible**: PT's Africa plan (turns 5–9) paid +1 dart every turn for the rest of the game and decided the win at the horn (16 vs 11). The banner made the cause visible every turn.
- **The clock endgame is genuinely tense**: the final two rounds were all-out attacks on the box leader (VOLT 14→10), reshuffling 2nd/3rd place on the last turn.
- **Weaker spots**: mid-game build-up turns (3× own-reinforce) are low-drama without commentary; the Arsenal is fun but rarely decisive when everyone just feeds homeland; long clocks (14+ turns/player) drag for eliminated-ish players (JO spent ~8 turns at 3–6 boxes).
