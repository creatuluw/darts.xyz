# how many territories are there on the classic risk board?

The classic **Risk** board has **42 territories**, grouped into **6 continents**: North America, South America, Europe, Africa, Asia, and Australia.[^1_1]

## Why it matters

Your baseline reinforcements are based on territories controlled: divide your number of territories by 3 and round down, with a minimum of 3 armies per turn. Continent control adds bonus armies on top.

# how many boxes are there on a dart board? (S/D/T)

A standard dartboard has **82 scoring boxes/areas** if you count every separately bounded scoring region:

- **40 single areas** — each of the 20 numbers has an outer single and an inner single region.
- **20 doubles** — the thin outer ring.
- **20 trebles/triples** — the thin inner ring.
- **2 bull areas** — outer bull (25) and inner bull / double bull (50).

So, using **S / D / T** notation:

$$
40S + 20D + 20T + 2\text{ bulls} = 82
$$

If you mean only the named targets **S1–S20, D1–D20, T1–T20**, that is **60 target labels**—but the singles physically appear in two separate boxes per number. The inner bull counts as a double in many formats, while the outer bull is usually treated separately. [^2_1][^2_2]
