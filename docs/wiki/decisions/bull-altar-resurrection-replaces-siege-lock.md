# Bull Altar resurrection replaces insurgents; Siege map B locked

## Context

Risk Darts brainstorm (see [[risk-darts-proposed-game-mode]]). Until now the standing baseline said: no elimination — a player who lost all land became an **insurgent** (keep throwing, any neutral wedge hit becomes the new homeland), and the combat model was still open (Question 4: glass / siege / fortress, with an assistant lean toward Siege B). User ruling this turn: when a player dies he may throw at the bullseye; if he hits, he can take any territory he wants.

## Decision

Adopt the **Bull Altar** resurrection package:

- **Bull is no longer a territory** — its only job is resurrection. Map becomes the 20 wedge territories across six continents.
- **Dead = 0 territories.** On your turn, throw all 3 darts at bull.
- **Outer bull (25):** resurrect — steal ANY territory on the map; it arrives at 1 HP (fragile, like any fresh conquest).
- **Inner bull (50):** resurrect — steal any territory at FULL 3 HP (the true miracle).
- **Miss:** stay dead, try again next turn. No penalty, just tension.

This **replaces the insurgent rule** locked in the Timed War decision — dead players no longer keep playing on the map.

Also **Siege map (B) is locked as the combat baseline** (Bull Altar requires conquest combat to exist): 3 HP per territory; single/double/treble = 1/2/3 damage (a treble on a full-HP wedge steals outright); hitting your own land reinforces +1 HP (cap 3); fresh conquests reset to 1 HP. Resolves Question 4.

## Why

- **Death becomes a threat vector, not a pity state.** A resurrected player will obviously steal from the leader, so every eliminated player returns as a guided missile aimed at first place — "the bigger your empire, the scarier your graveyard." The leader now defends against the living AND the dead.
- Gives the weakest player the single most dramatic moment available — ideal for the mixed shark+casual table this mode targets.
- Odds: 3 darts at outer bull revives a casual in ~1–2 dead turns, so the graveyard empties instead of snowballing.

## Alternatives considered

- **Insurgents** (standing until now): dead players keep throwing at the map — less drama, no graveyard-aims-at-the-leader pressure.
- **Hard elimination:** dead players sit out; wastes their night, breaks "everyone matters until the end".
- **Flat resurrection (no 25/50 tier):** loses the inner-bull full-HP steal as a distinct miracle moment.

## Consequences / open items

- **Open — Question 5:** darts left in hand after a successful resurrection — A) turn ends (savor the drama) vs B) keep throwing (leftover darts attack as normal). Assistant recommendation: **B**.
- Timed War scoring previously awarded **+2 for the bull** as a standalone capital; with the bull repurposed as the altar that bonus is obsolete — endgame scoring needs a pass when next revisited.
