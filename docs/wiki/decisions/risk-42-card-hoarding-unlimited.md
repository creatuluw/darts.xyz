# Risk 42 card hoarding is unlimited — no mandatory cash-in at 5+ cards

## Context

The territory-cards decision (2026-10-23) left one sub-question open: is cashing-in **mandatory at 5+ cards in hand** (recommended YES, mirroring real Risk) or is hoarding fully free? Real Risk forces a cash-in to prevent runaway hands.

## The user's ruling

2026-10-23, kicking off the income review: **"je mag onbeperkt hamsteren van kaarten"** — unlimited card hoarding. Answer: **free hoarding**.

## Decision

- **No hand limit, no forced cash-in at 5+ cards.** A player may hold as many cards as they conquer and cash sets whenever they choose during their own turn.
- The earlier balance concern (a 6+ card double mega-payout in one turn) is **accepted by the user** rather than engineered away; the finite 42-card deck and per-set escalation already bound late-game payouts.

## Consequences

- The territory-cards decision's open sub-question is closed — no 5-card trigger exists anywhere in the cash-in handler or UI.
- Hand-size UI shows the full hand with no warning threshold or forced-cash modal.
- Deliberate asymmetry with real Risk; don't "fix" it back later without asking.
