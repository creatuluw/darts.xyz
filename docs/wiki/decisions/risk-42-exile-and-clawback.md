# Risk 42 elimination: exile & clawback — zero boxes ≠ death

## Context

Risk 42 design Q&A (2026-09-09). With the mirror-damage attack rule, a player can be ground down to **zero boxes**. Q8 asked what losing your last territory means: true elimination, or something softer. The user locked **B**.

## Decision

**Exile & clawback — zero boxes ≠ dead:**

- An exiled player (0 territories) **keeps their seat and their 3 darts per turn** — they stay in rotation.
- **Recapture at 0 rebuilds you at 1 army** — claw back any blank/enemy territory with a dart and you're back on the board.
- Death costs you your **defensive income**, not your seat: no boxes = no income, but you can always shoot your way back in.
- **No new machinery** — reuses the existing claim verb; no respawn rules, no special-case UI.

Alternatives (offered as A/C/D in the Q&A, only B's text was relayed in the lock) are superseded by this ruling.

## Rationale

- Both conquest modes now land on **resurrection over elimination** — Trebles & Territories has the Bull Altar resurrection / Last Stand amendment; Risk 42 has exile & clawback. Party darts favors nobody sitting out.
- It's the minimal rule: income starvation is already emergent from owning nothing; the seat-stays is the only added clause.

## Consequences

- Game length is bounded by the endgame clock (Domination headline / 170-301-501 turns), not by elimination — players can be reduced but never removed.
- Shark-check: exiling a leader doesn't eject them; you must keep their recapture targets contested.
