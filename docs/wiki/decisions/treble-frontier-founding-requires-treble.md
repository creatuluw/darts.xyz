# Treble-frontier founding: blank territories require a treble to claim

## Context

Resolves standing **Question 6** ("how does the map start?") on the [Risk Darts proposed game mode](../pages/concepts/risk-darts-proposed-game-mode.md) concept page. The standing baseline said "hitting neutral land = claiming it"; Question 6 offered cold start, founding dart, or snake draft. User ruling this turn: **the first claim on a territory always needs to be a treble — otherwise it's nobody's.**

## Decision

- A blank wedge can only be **founded by a treble**; treble-founded land arrives at full 3 HP.
- Singles and doubles into blank wedges are **dead darts** — strict reading, shipped strict.
- Exception: the **Bull Altar** resurrection still claims a blank at 1 HP without a treble — the altar stays the landless player's back door.
- Replaces the "neutral land = claiming it" baseline clause and rules out Question 6's founding-dart and snake-draft options.

## Rationale

- The opening becomes a **treble rush** — the map gets civilized slowly and violently; frontier tension.
- Asymmetric difficulty creates a **role split with one rule**: founding needs a treble (~2–25% by skill) but robbing owned land needs only singles (3 HP, 1/2/3 damage) → skilled players become settlers building empires, casuals become raiders looting them. Solves the mixed-crowd problem without handicap mechanics.
- Effort = grip: treble-founded 3 HP, stolen/bull-claimed 1 HP — consistent and intuitive.

## Alternatives considered

- **Free claims on any hit (cold start)** — rejected: no frontier, no role split.
- **Founding dart / snake draft pre-war** — ruled out by the user's treble rule.

## Consequences

- Early-game singles into blank wedges are wasted darts. Flag for playtesting: revisit only if casuals feel useless early — strictness is what makes the frontier feel like a frontier.
- Combat vs owned land (siege map B) is unchanged; only blank-land founding changed.
